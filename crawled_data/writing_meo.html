Bộ Form Master Writing Aptis (Full 4 Parts) — Chuẩn Band B2+

Hệ thống khung mẫu chuẩn hóa áp dụng cho 100% tất cả 40 bộ đề thi Writing trong cơ sở dữ liệu AptisKey. Thay nội dung trong các ô [ngoặc vuông] để hoàn thành bài thi dễ dàng.

Đạt chuẩn Band B2 CEFR (Tối ưu tiêu chí Task Achievement, Cohesion, Range & Accuracy của British Council)

Bài thi Aptis ESOL Writing kéo dài 50 phút gồm 4 phần. Bạn chỉ cần học thuộc cấu trúc khung này và thay thế các từ khóa thuộc chủ đề CLB đề bài yêu cầu.

Part 1: Short Answers (1 – 5 từ / câu)

Quy tắc Part 1: Trả lời 5 câu hỏi cá nhân ngắn gọn. Không viết thành đoạn văn dài. Đảm bảo chính xác chính tả và cấu trúc 1-5 từ.

P1 Mẫu câu trả lời đa năng Part 1
1. Sức khỏe / Cảm xúc (How are you?): ➜ I am doing great, thanks! / I am feeling fine, thank you. 2. Thời tiết / Mùa (What's the weather / favorite season?): ➜ It is sunny and warm today. / I love the spring season. 3. Sở thích (Favorite sport / music / food / movie?): ➜ I enjoy playing football. / I love listening to pop music. 4. Trang phục / Thói quen (What are you wearing / do in evening?): ➜ I am wearing a T-shirt. / I usually watch TV shows. 5. Địa điểm / Dự định (Where do you want to go on holiday?): ➜ I want to visit Japan. / I usually go with my family.

Part 2: Form Filling (20 – 30 từ)

Quy tắc Part 2: Viết 1 đoạn văn duy nhất (2-3 câu). Nêu rõ lý do gia nhập CLB hoặc đối tượng / sở thích liên quan đến chủ đề CLB.

P2 Form 1: Lý do gia nhập CLB (Why do you want to join?)
I am really excited to join the [Tên CLB - ví dụ: Art Club] because I want to improve my knowledge and share my passion for [Chủ đề CLB - ví dụ: painting] with other members. Moreover, it is a great opportunity to make new friends.
P2 Form 2: Mô tả sở thích / Trải nghiệm liên quan
I have been passionate about [Chủ đề CLB - ví dụ: reading books] since I was young. I usually spend my free time doing this activity, which helps me reduce stress and relax after a busy working day.

Part 3: Social Network Chat (3 câu - 30 tới 40 từ/câu)

Quy tắc Part 3: Tương tác với 3 thành viên nhóm chat CLB. Mỗi câu trả lời đạt chuẩn 30-40 từ, sử dụng liên từ mạch lạc.

P3 Master Form trả lời 3 thành viên chat
👤 Member 1 (Kỷ niệm / Trải nghiệm quá khứ): Hi [Tên Member 1]! To be honest, I had a very similar experience last year. I [động từ quá khứ: visited / bought / participated in...] and it was really unforgettable. It helped me learn a lot of valuable things about [Chủ đề CLB]. What about your experience? 👤 Member 2 (Hỏi lời khuyên / Lựa chọn A hay B): Hi [Tên Member 2]! In my opinion, you should choose [Lựa chọn A / take a course...] because it is much more convenient and effective. In addition, it helps you save a lot of time and money in the long run. Hope my advice helps! 👤 Member 3 (Quan điểm / Tranh luận xã hội): Hi [Tên Member 3]! From my point of view, I completely agree with this idea. On the one hand, [Lý do 1: it brings many benefits to people]. On the other hand, we should also consider [Lý do 2]. Therefore, finding a balance is very important.

Part 4: Email Writing (Informal & Formal)

Q4 phản hồi thông báo của CLB. Gồm 1 email thân mật gửi bạn cùng CLB (≈50 từ) và 1 email trang trọng gửi quản lý / Chủ tịch CLB (120–150 từ).

Part 4.1: Informal Email gửi Bạn Thân (≈50 từ)
Hi [Tên người bạn], Have you read the latest notice from our [Tên CLB]? I feel really [surprised / disappointed / excited] to hear that [tóm tắt thông báo: the club might be closed / the fee will increase]. I'm planning to write an email to the president to express our concerns and suggest some ideas. What do you think about this? Let me know soon! Best, [Tên bạn]
Part 4.2: Formal Email gửi Chủ tịch / Quản lý CLB (120–150 từ)
Dear President, I am writing this email to express my feelings and suggestions regarding the recent announcement about [tóm tắt thông báo: the proposal to demolish old buildings / the club closure / the change of venue]. First of all, I was quite [surprised / concerned / delighted] upon receiving this news. As a dedicated member of the club, I believe that this decision will significantly affect our regular activities as well as the members' overall experience. To address this issue effectively, I would like to propose a few practical solutions. Firstly, we could [Gợi ý 1: organize a meeting to gather feedback from all members / host a fundraising event]. Secondly, it would be beneficial to [Gợi ý 2: promote the club on social media to attract new participants]. Furthermore, I am more than willing to volunteer and support the committee in executing these plans. I hope you will take my suggestions into consideration. I look forward to hearing your response soon. Yours sincerely, [Họ và tên của bạn]
Gợi ý từ vựng đánh giá & Cảm xúc (B2 Level)
Đánh giá tính chất kế hoạch (Khách quan)

Tích cực excellent, promising, practical, meaningful, useful, valuable, beneficial, inspiring, innovative, effective, important, necessary

Tiêu cực unclear, unrealistic, unnecessary, unhelpful, confusing, ineffective, disappointing, problematic, risky

Bộc lộ cảm xúc cá nhân

Tích cực excited, pleased, satisfied, encouraged, motivated, happy, hopeful, confident, inspired, proud

Tiêu cực worried, concerned, disappointed, confused, surprised, upset, uncertain

Ví dụ thực tế ráp Form: Book Club & Art Club

Đề bài mẫu (Book Club - Q4):
Our club wants to organize an event for the public by inviting a famous author as a speaker. What kind of author do you suggest? What topic should the speaker speak on?
1. Write an email to your friend (50 words).
2. Write an email to the manager of the club (120–150 words).
Ví dụ Part 4.1: Email cho bạn (≈50 từ)
Hi Minh, Have you read the latest notice from our Book Club? I feel really excited to hear that we are organizing a public talk with a famous author. I'm planning to write an email to the president to express our concerns and suggest some ideas. What do you think about this? Let me know soon! Best, An
Ví dụ Part 4.2: Email cho quản lý (120–150 từ)
Dear President, I am writing this email to express my feelings and suggestions regarding the recent announcement about the club's plan to organize a public talk with a famous author. First of all, I was quite delighted upon receiving this news. As a dedicated member of the club, I believe that this decision will significantly affect our regular activities as well as the members' overall experience. To address this issue effectively, I would like to propose a few practical solutions. Firstly, we could invite a bestselling contemporary novelist to talk about modern literature. Secondly, it would be beneficial to promote the event on social media to attract more young readers and students. Furthermore, I am more than willing to volunteer and support the committee in executing these plans. I hope you will take my suggestions into consideration. I look forward to hearing your response soon. Yours sincerely, An Nguyen
